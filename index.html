SAEmnesia: Supervised Sparse Autoencoder Finetuning for Concept Unlearning in Diffusion Models

Enrico Cassano   Riccardo Renzulli   Marco Nurisso   Mirko Zaffaroni   Alan Perotti   Marco Grangetto

Paper Code Model (HuggingFace)

Abstract

SAEmnesia fine-tunes a pre-trained Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) with a supervised cross-entropy objective that assigns dedicated latent neurons to specific concepts. At inference time, suppressing those neurons selectively erases the target concept from the diffusion model's generation, without retraining the diffusion model itself. Standard SAE-based unlearning (e.g., SAeUron) relies on unsupervised latent features that may be entangled with multiple concepts. SAEmnesia supervises the SAE with a concept-assignment loss during finetuning, enforcing that a dedicated set of latent neurons fires exclusively for the concept to be erased. At inference time, steering those neurons toward zero suppresses the concept cleanly.
  • No diffusion model retraining — only the SAE weights are updated
  • Concept-specific latent assignment via cross-entropy supervision
  • Evaluated on the UnlearnCanvas benchmark (objects & styles)
  • Robustness tested against adversarial re-learning attacks
